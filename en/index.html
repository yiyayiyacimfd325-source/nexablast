The goal

Climb in. Have the last word.

Pick one of eight war mechs, fit your weapons and drop into short, intense arena matches. Depending on the mode you win by scoring the most, reaching the target first or being the last one standing. Every match earns credits and XP to upgrade your mech and unlock new ones.

  • Matches last minutes: always time for one more
  • Every mech has its own weapons and signature ability
  • No forced ads: an ad plays only when you choose a reward
  • Try every locked mech in the training ground

Game modes

Six arena modes

Quick Battle for a short break, Team Battle with friends, Last Man Standing for pure nerves.

Quick Battle

3 minutes, 2-4 pilots. Bots fill the empty slots.

Free-For-All

Up to 8 pilots. First to 25 kills, or the best after 6 minutes, wins.

Team Battle

Up to 4 vs 4. The first team to 15 kills wins.

Respawn Deathmatch

10 minutes of fast respawns. Highest score wins.

Last Man Standing

No respawns; the arena shrinks after 2 minutes.

Duel

One on one, best of three rounds.

Features

Solo or with friends

Online and parties

Matches of up to 8 pilots, 6-letter room codes, quick match and parties of 4. One account on phone, PC and browser.

Voice chat

Talk in your party, the room lobby and the match. Push to talk or always on, and mute any pilot with one tap.

Ranked leagues

3 vs 3 ranked matches and 8 leagues from Iron to Challenger, with a reward the first time you reach each one.

Story campaign

8 chapters, 40 missions and 8 bosses, with briefings voiced in English and Turkish.

Clans and events

Clan events, a weekly clan war, tournaments and a new arena rule every week.

Season pass and rewards

6-week seasons with 50 tiers, daily and weekly missions, and 65 achievements.

Controller and touch

Full controller support, rebindable keys, aim assist and adjustable touch controls.

Accessibility

3 colour-blind modes, 4 text sizes, reduced flashes, and screen shake and vibration you can switch off.
